# Generator architecture and deterministic build

## Entry points

`tools/build_city.py` is the CLI; `citygen.pipeline.build_city` coordinates the modules and exports the MTA resource. Every procedural subsystem accepts a seed or derives a stable per-object seed. Rebuilds overwrite the same named outputs. `--clean` reads `data/build_manifest.json` and deletes only files listed as generated by the previous build; hand-authored Lua and shader source are preserved.

`tools/validate_city.py` runs checks over the generated resource. `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v` runs fast unit and integration checks.

## Stages

1. `config.py` defines the origin, 768 m square footprint, 192 m tile size, and budgets.
2. `road_graph.py` builds the directed, elevation-aware graph; road segments carry class, lane count, direction, speed, width, controls, grade/alignment, and tags. The current graph has 44 nodes, 64 edges, and one connected component.
3. `terrain.py` constructs the island shelf, rolling terrain, creek bed, road flattening, tunnel openings, and high/low surfaces/collision. `pipeline.py` adds graded flat pads under buildings and the surface lot, blending each pad into the surrounding terrain; parcel-level collision is supplied by the building/parking shell.
4. `roads.py`, `intersections.py`, `bridges.py`, `tunnels.py`, and `highways.py` generate drivable ribbons, junctions, markings, ramps, structure, and collision.
5. `districts.py` deterministically places archetypes and land-use features. `buildings.py` makes varied massing/facades/roof detail; `landuse.py`, `props.py`, `infrastructure.py`, and `vegetation.py` add the park, parking, furniture, street hardware, lights, and blocked-area-aware planting.
6. `textures.py`, `weather_assets.py`, and `lighting_assets.py` author material maps/sprites; `renderware.py` serializes DFF/TXD/COL3; `pipeline.py` splits visuals/collision into local-coordinate tiles and exports JSON, audio, `meta.xml`, registry and budgets.
7. `validator.py` checks resource references, file structure, mesh/texture indices, collision offsets, geometry clearance, shader/Lua lexical structure, and limits.

## Coordinate and tile rules

Generator coordinates are local to the city. World XY = `(origin_x + local_x, origin_y + local_y)`; tile-object Z is `origin_z`, with model coordinates containing the local terrain elevation. The default origin is `(2600, 0, 0)`. Four tiles per axis cover local `[-384,384]` and have centers at `-288, -96, 96, 288`.

Faces are assigned to a tile by their centroid and retain their complete triangle. A face crossing a tile boundary can therefore overhang its tile object's nominal half-size by the face width; geometry is not clipped or duplicated. The current tile content and DFF/COL coordinate ranges are checked by the exporter and binary validator. If future assets grow past the COL3 signed fixed-point range (about ±256 local units), decrease tile size or subdivide them further.

The high and low tiers share tree placements and building archetypes. Low geometry retains large road/intersection/bridge/tunnel silhouettes, reduces terrain/vegetation detail, and removes small road markings and street furniture. Models are merged per tile to keep object counts modest. The 16 high and 16 low object assets are loaded during resource start; MTA's distance/LOD handling controls visible model tier. This is tile-based LOD, not on-demand DFF downloading or spatial rebuilding.

## Road/building placement

`validate_layout` checks graph connectivity and edge attributes, building/feature overlap, building pair overlap, road-envelope clearance, and land-mask placement. Road checks sample centerlines against rotated AABBs and allow grade-separated freeway/ramp segments above or below a building. This is a deterministic generator guard, not a substitute for driving the intersections in MTA.

## Extend the vertical slice

- Add a node/segment or a subsystem to the graph before adding route geometry; keep intersections connected.
- Add building placement rows with archetype/facade IDs rather than hand-building a new export path.
- Use `blocked_areas` for tree exclusion around any new structure/parking footprint.
- Preserve local transforms and model-name/material naming across DFF, TXD, runtime JSON and `meta.xml`.
- Keep new dynamic effects inside the documented MTA/DX9 capability boundary.
- Add tests for new geometry and its clearance/budget cost before expanding the city footprint.
